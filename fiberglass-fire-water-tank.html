Fiberglass Fire Water Tank Manufacturer in UAE

In the UAE, we manufacture fiberglass fire water tanks for commercial, industrial, residential, and infrastructure projects that require a dedicated water reserve for a firefighting system.

The tanks are constructed from corrosion-resistant FRP and are suitable for storing firefighting water for sprinkler systems, fire hydrants, hose reels, and other emergency fire protection applications.

Options to choose from Fiberglass Fire Water Tanks

  • Horizontal & Vertical layouts
  • Above-ground configurations
  • Environments underground
  • Tailored size and capacity
  • Special inlet & outlet connections
  • Access and inspection openings
  • Project-specific nozzles and accessories

What are Fiberglass Fire Water Tanks Used For?

  • Commercial buildings
  • Storage facilities
  • Factories and Industrial Buildings
  • Hospitals and hotels
  • Buildings for public use and schools
  • Fire protection system

Reliable Fire Protection Water Supply

Water tanks are meant to hold a dedicated reserve of water for emergency use. The FRP construction provides the tank with rust and corrosion protection for long-term water storage in demanding environments.

Its lightweight also simplifies transportation and installation compared to many conventional tank materials.

Custom Production of Fiberglass Reinforced Plastic Fire Water Tanks

We manufacture fire water tanks in fiberglass to your required capacity, size, type of installation, inlet & outlet size, overflow connection, drain connection, maintenance hole size, and project layout.

Each tank can be designed to meet the requirements of available space and the fire protection system.

Engineered for UAE Conditions

Our Fiberglass Fire Water Tanks are built to withstand the harsh UAE climate, high temperatures, and humidity, and are suitable for indoor or outdoor installation.

Fire water can be stored long-term, and the FRP construction is corrosion-resistant and needs little maintenance.

We are a manufacturer & supplier of fiberglass fire water tanks for projects all over the UAE.
